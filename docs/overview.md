# Project Overview: Professional AI Coaching Engineering

MetaOpenCoach is an industrial-grade application generation ecosystem designed for coaches, athletes, and performance professionals who demand computational power, data sovereignty, and technological independence.

## TL;DR
MetaOpenCoach is a methodology-to-code transformation engine that turns your professional coaching manuals (PDF/DOCX) into a standalone, private, and encrypted software application. It bridges the gap between a coach's expertise and an athlete's screen without relying on mandatory cloud infrastructure.

## The "Chef & Recipe" Analogy

To understand MetaOpenCoach, think of it like a high-end restaurant:

- **The Chef (Template Core)**: This is the base software engine. It knows how to manage a kitchen (server), serve clients (front-end), and handle data. By itself, it has no menu.
- **The Recipe Book (Your Methodology)**: This is your PDF or document where you explain your coaching method (e.g., strength protocols, nutrition rules).
- **The Sous-Chef (Python Orchestrator)**: This assistant reads your recipe book, creates a custom menu, and "teaches" the Chef exactly how to execute your specific method.
- **The Final Restaurant (Generated EXE)**: The result is a specialized, standalone restaurant (executable) that only serves your elite recipes, operating completely independently and privately.

## Data Sovereignty by Design

Unlike generic AI wrappers, MetaOpenCoach prioritizes **Data Sovereignty**:
- **Local-First**: Patient and athlete data reside in a local encrypted vault, not a third-party cloud.
- **AI-Agnostic**: Use local LLMs via LM Studio/Ollama for 100% offline operations, or connect to high-performance cloud providers.
- **Sovereign Storage**: Advanced AES-256 encryption ensures that even if the hardware is accessed, the data remain protected.

## Core Capabilities

- **App Builder UI**: A professional graphical interface (`app_builder_ui.py`) to manage methodology ingestion and AI configuration.
- **Standalone Binaries**: Distribute your coaching method as a single `.exe` file that requires zero installation from the athlete.
- **Methodology Injection**: Your specific knowledge is distilled into the AI's core logic, eliminating generic "hallucinations."
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Monitor athlete adherence and churn risk via a modern, responsive dashboard.
